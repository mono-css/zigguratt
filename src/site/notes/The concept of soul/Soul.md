Most of the souls are made of 5 parts:
Ren - name, identity 
Bu - personality 
Shu - shadow, past self
Ib - heart, empathy and moral
Hazz / ḥaẓẓa - luck, fortune

These parts are held together by a physical [[The concept of soul/Body (Khet)\|Body (Khet)]]. Once Khet is not present because of death, trading, selling, etc. there is nothing holding the soul together and it becomes unstable.
