# Soul

A soul is an entity that is capable of thinking, talking and interacting with the world around it.

Most of the souls are made of 5 soul fragments:
**Ren** - name, identity, destiny
**Bu** - personality 
**Shu** - shadow, past self
**Ib** - heart, empathy and moral
**Hazz** / **ḥaẓẓa** - luck, fortune

These soul fragments must be held together by a [[2 The concept of soul/Body (Khet)\|Body (Khet)]] otherwise they are highly unstable and there is a risk of soul fragments detaching from the soul. Single soul fragments and not capable of functioning on its own and it has only an instinct to go to [[1 Ziggurat/Kṣetra\|Kṣetra]].
